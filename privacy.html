Privacy information

This page explains the information sent when you make an enquiry through this website.

Contacting us

The form asks for your name, phone number and details of the work. You can also provide an email address and town or postcode. These details are used to respond to your enquiry and discuss the job.

Enquiry form provider

The form sends your details through FormSubmit to JF Stone & Build. Read FormSubmit’s privacy policy for information about its handling of data. You can also contact us directly by phone or email.

Google tag

The homepage and enquiry confirmation page use a Google Ads tag for website measurement. Google may process information about your visit. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Questions about your information

For questions about an enquiry or a request concerning your personal information, email jfstoneandbuild@gmail.com or call 07777 142122.
